# Popover

## Purpose and anatomy

Popover presents small contextual supporting content anchored to a required trigger. Content is required and may contain short text or a small number of interactive controls. `PopoverClose` is optional.

## Behaviour and accessibility

Radix manages focus, Escape and outside-click dismissal, positioning, and focus return. The trigger must have an accessible name. Interactive controls retain their normal keyboard behaviour.

## Usage guidance

Use Popover for a compact contextual interaction that benefits from staying near its trigger. Tooltip is for non-interactive supplementary text; DropdownMenu is for a compact action list. Use Dialog or Sheet when a task needs structure, sustained focus, or more room.

Do not use Popover for complex forms, long content, destructive confirmation, or page-level tasks.
